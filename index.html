Junior Research Retreat in Mathematics

If Only This Worked…

A workshop on number theory and representation theory
Dates: September 27 – October 2, 2026  ·  Venue: Hotel Seemöwe, Eifel region, Germany

About

The workshop will provide a low-pressure atmosphere for young researchers working on number theory and representation theory to talk about projects that they made progress on but did not finish, with the aim of sparking new collaborations which take them in new, fruitful directions. The mathematical topic is the Langlands program, which studies deep connections between number theory and representation theory.

Format

The main focus of the retreat is collaboration. We will only have talks on the first three days and, rather than a few long talks, will have talks lasting 30 minutes (speakers can give more than one talk). The first three days will consist of six 30-minute talks in the morning and early afternoon, breaks for coffee and lunch, and three hours of collaboration and discussion time in the later afternoon. The mathematical component of the last two days will be entirely focused on collaboration and discussion.
