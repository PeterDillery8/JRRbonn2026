If Only This Worked…

About

During this Junior Research Retreat, PhD students and postdocs in number theory and representation theory will share promising ideas they have worked on that didn't quite work out, and attempt to revive them with other participants.

Format

The main focus of the retreat is low-stakes, relaxed collaboration. During the first two days, participants will give 30-minute pitches of their project ideas. The rest of the time will be spent in small groups, working on the ideas presented in the talks. Breakfast, coffee breaks, lunch, and dinner will be provided for all participants.
